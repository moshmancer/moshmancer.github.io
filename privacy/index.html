Privacy Policy

Developer: Moshmancer · Contact: 0x.moshmancer@gmail.com · Last updated: 2026-09-13

Data stored on your device

Moshmancer stores connection profiles, host names, user names, ports, known-host fingerprints, encrypted passwords, SSH private keys, terminal session checkpoints, and optional terminal transcripts in the app's private storage. Passwords are encrypted with Android Keystore. Terminal transcripts and session checkpoints are encrypted before being written.

Network connections

The app connects only to hosts that you configure. The current release does not include analytics, advertising, crash telemetry, cloud sync, or a Moshmancer remote account. SSH, ET, Mosh, and Herdr traffic goes directly to the hosts you choose. Your network or VPN provider may still observe connection metadata.

Clipboard, files, and notifications

Moshmancer reads the clipboard or a selected file only when you explicitly tap a paste, import, or choose-file action. Imported content is stored locally as a connection profile and is not uploaded by Moshmancer. Foreground-service notifications are generated locally on your device.

Data sharing and retention

Moshmancer does not sell or share your connection data. Data remains on your device until you delete a connection, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Security

No storage or network method is perfect. Use SSH keys where possible, protect your device with a screen lock, keep your hosts patched, and connect only to systems you trust.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Questions about privacy can be sent to 0x.moshmancer@gmail.com.
